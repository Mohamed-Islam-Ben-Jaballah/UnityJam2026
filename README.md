# Dice-Drive

> A roguelike deck-builder where every roll of the dice decides which cards you
> can play. Roll. Pick. Drag. Survive.

---

## Welcome

Welcome travelers and heroes of all the facets of the earth! Are you ready to
roll your dice and throw your cards? Let fate spin you on a journey across
plates — only if you are brave enough.

## The Game in One Minute

You hold a hand of cards. Each turn you roll your dice **one at a time**, and
the number you roll unlocks which cards you may play. Pick a card, drag it onto
the map, and watch it move, strike, jump, or reshape the earth itself. Cross the
dungeon, grab the treasure, and reach the exit before the enemies — and the
ground beneath you — claim you.

---

## How to Play

### Step 1 — Roll the dice

You carry a set of dice: a d4, a d6, a d8, and more as you grow. On your turn
you throw them **one by one**. Each result is a fresh chance to act.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: the dice rolling / dice UI -->

### Step 2 — Pick a card

The number you just rolled lights up certain cards in your hand. Playable cards
glow — everything else stays dark for now. Choose wisely: you play **one card
per die roll**.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: hand of cards with playable cards highlighted -->

### Step 3 — Drag it to an area of use

Drag the card over the map. The tiles where it can act light up around your
character. Drop it on a highlighted tile to commit.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: dragging a card, valid tiles highlighted -->

### Step 4 — Watch the action

The card resolves instantly — your hero walks, leaps, strikes, heals, clears a
blocked path, or floods the ground with sand. Then it is the next die's turn.

## Lucky Numbers & the Classes

Normal cards are **always** playable. Every other class answers to a single
**lucky number** — roll it, and that class awakens.

| Roll | Awakens |
|------|---------|
| 1    | Angel |
| 2    | Normal only |
| 3    | Elf |
| 4    | Normal only |
| 5    | Desert |
| 6    | Devil |
| 7    | Normal only |
| 8    | China |

- **Normal** — your faithful Walk; move a number of tiles equal to your roll.
- **Elf (3)** — Leapol (jump), Squimi (damage), Heilin (heal).
- **Devil (6)** — Qa'faz (jump), Sara'akh (damage), Ha'yen (remove obstacle).
- **China (8)** — Maoyi (jump), Sunhai (damage), Qiahni (draw 2 cards).
- **Angel (1)** — Seraphil (deal damage in a cross around you).
- **Desert (5)** — Zulfar (flood the 8 tiles around you with sand).

> Grow your dice set to reach higher numbers — a d6 unlocks the Devil, a d8
> unlocks China.

## Your Goal

Reach the **exit** of each dungeon. On the way, collect **treasure** to grow
stronger and **defeat the enemies** that stand in your path.

- **Treasure** becomes gold — spend it between maps to heal, trim your deck, or
  reroll new cards.
- **Enemies** hold their ground. They telegraph an attack on one turn, then
  strike on the next. Move out of range, or cut them down first.
- **Holes** are instant death — you restart the level. **Obstacles** block,
  wound, or cannot be passed at all. Use Jump, Ha'yen, or a well-aimed strike.
- **Between maps** you open a pack of new cards, remove up to three you no
  longer want, and upgrade your dice.

Get stronger. Go deeper. Reach the exit.
